In partnership with the Peruvian government, this project evaluates a new early childhood education model providing extended-hour childcare with trained educators. The evaluation uses randomized assignment of slots in oversubscribed centers and tracks impacts on child development, maternal labor supply, and household well-being. Data collection is ongoing, with follow-up surveys and long-term administrative tracking planned to assess sustained effects.
