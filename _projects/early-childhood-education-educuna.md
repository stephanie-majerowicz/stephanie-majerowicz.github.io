In partnership with the Peruvian government, this project evaluates a new early childhood education model, Educuna, providing extended-hour childcare with trained educators. The evaluation uses randomized assignment of slots in oversubscribed centers and tracks impacts on child development, maternal labor supply, and household well-being.
